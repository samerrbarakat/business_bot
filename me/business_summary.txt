Maison Noisette — French technique, Lebanese soul

Maison Noisette is a French-Lebanese pâtisserie on Armenia Street in Mar Mikhael, Beirut, founded in 2021. Our mission is to make classic French pastry every day with the flavors and ingredients of Lebanon (Chouf honey, Batroun lemons, orange blossom, rose and local pistachios) while wasting nothing. We are open Tuesday to Sunday, 7:30 to 19:30, and closed on Mondays.

We offer four services. Our boutique sells fresh pastries daily, such as butter and za'atar croissants, pistachio-rose éclairs, orange-blossom ashta millefeuille, Batroun lemon tarts and macaron boxes. We design custom celebration cakes from $45 (serves 8–10) with at least 72 hours' notice; wedding cakes are by consultation only and priced on request. We cater dessert tables for corporate and private events of 30 to 300 guests, priced on request after a short brief. On Saturday mornings we run hands-on pastry workshops for $45 per person, with a maximum of 8 people. We deliver within Beirut: delivery is free on orders over $40 and otherwise costs $4, and orders placed before 2 pm can be delivered the same day.

The team is led by co-founder and Head Pâtissière Nour Haddad, who spent eight years in Parisian pastry kitchens. Co-founder Karim Saab runs operations, delivery and catering. Cake designer Maya Khoury, who has a fine-arts background, designs every custom and wedding cake.

What makes us different: French precision with Lebanese flavors you will not find elsewhere, custom cakes designed by an artist rather than picked from a catalogue, and a zero-waste promise. Unsold pastries go to a local food-rescue partner every evening, and all our packaging is compostable. For custom orders, catering or private workshops, our team replies within one business day at hello@maisonnoisette.lb.
